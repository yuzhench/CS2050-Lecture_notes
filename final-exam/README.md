## What to expect

The exam is designed to measure students' engagement with the course over the full semester. Unless specifically excluded, any material from the lectures, quizzes, and homework assignments is admissible. However, we suggest focusing your studying on the topics listed below, as well as the quizzes and reading assignments.

The exam format will be similar to the quiz format, consisting mostly of multiple choice and short answer questions. However, unlike the quizzes, the exam is fully "closed book," meaning no references (paper or electronic) are permitted. For this reason, the exam will be more difficult than the quizzes.

We will not require detailed mathematical manipulations.
